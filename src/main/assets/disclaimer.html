Privacy

FlightIntel uses your device's location to find nearby airports and weather stations. Your location is processed on your device, and your coordinates are not sent anywhere.

To show weather, charts, TFRs and maps, the app downloads data from public sources, including the FAA, the National Weather Service (aviationweather.gov), OpenStreetMap and api.flightintel.com. As with any download, these servers see your IP address and what you request, such as a nearby weather station. If you turn on alerts, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a token so alerts can reach it.

FlightIntel has no accounts, ads or analytics, and does not collect personal information.
