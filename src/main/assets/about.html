Data Sources

Airports and facilities — FAA National Flight Data Center (NFDC), from its 28-day NASR subscription.

Charts and procedures — FAA Aeronautical Information Services: the Chart Supplement, Terminal Procedures (d-TPP) and the Digital Obstacle File.

Weather — National Weather Service Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov).

TFRs — FAA (tfr.faa.gov).

Maps — © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Time zones — GeoNames.

FlightIntel is not affiliated with the FAA or any other government agency. Always check official sources before flight.

Privacy

FlightIntel uses your device's location to find nearby airports and weather stations. Your location is processed on your device, and your coordinates are not sent anywhere.

To show weather, charts, TFRs and maps, the app downloads data from the sources above and from api.flightintel.com. As with any download, these servers see your IP address and what you request, such as a nearby weather station. If you turn on alerts, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your device a token so alerts can reach it.

FlightIntel has no accounts, ads or analytics, and does not collect personal information.
